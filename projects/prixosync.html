About This Project

PrixoSync is an enterprise multi-tenant SaaS business management platform built with tenant isolation at its core, deployed publicly to both the Google Play Store and Apple App Store. It gives teams a single, synchronized workspace to manage roles, submissions, job sites, and users across an organization.

Key Features

  • Multi-tenant architecture with tenant isolation
  • Role-based access and user management
  • Guided multi-step submission workflow
  • Job site tracking with photo documentation
  • Cross-platform deployment on Android and iOS
