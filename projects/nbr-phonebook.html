About This Project

NBR Phonebook is a government officer directory app for the National Board of Revenue, built to make finding the right officer fast and simple. It organizes contacts by department, zone, and office, with powerful search and advanced filtering to narrow results by name, designation, or office.

Key Features

  • Complete officer directory by department and zone
  • Fast search by name or designation
  • Advanced filtering (retirement date, birth date, office)
  • Detailed officer profiles with contact and office info
  • REST API-backed, always up to date
