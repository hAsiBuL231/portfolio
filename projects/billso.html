About This Project

Billso is an invoicing and billing app built for freelancers and small businesses. It lets you generate invoices and quotations in seconds, choose from a variety of professional templates, manage clients and items, and export polished, ready-to-send PDFs — all from your phone.

Key Features

  • Fast invoice & quotation generation
  • Multiple customizable invoice templates
  • Client and item records management
  • One-tap PDF export & sharing
  • Invoice status tracking (Paid, Pending, Overdue, Draft)
