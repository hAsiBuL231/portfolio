About This Project

Passport Photo Maker helps you create passport, visa, ID, and document photos from your phone. Choose a country or document size template, crop and edit the image, adjust the background, and export a high-resolution photo ready to print or share.

Key Features

  • Passport, visa, ID, and document photo templates for 150+ countries
  • Crop, rotate, and adjust brightness, contrast, and sharpness
  • AI-powered background removal and replacement
  • Printable multi-photo layouts for home or photo-lab printing
  • High-resolution export, share, and save to My Creations
