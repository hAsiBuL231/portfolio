About This Project

eMistry is a mobility-style marketplace that connects customers with mistries, mechanics, and service agents for home electric, electronics, plumbing, sanitary, car, and water-tank services, similar in concept to how ride-hailing apps match riders with nearby drivers by vehicle type. It ships as two dedicated apps — one for customers and one for service providers — backed by an admin dashboard for pricing, billing, and oversight.

I took over this project — the customer app, the provider app, the admin web dashboard, and the backend — from a team of five previous developers, and have been rebuilding and driving it forward solo since. It's currently under active development.

Key Features

  • Browse nearby service agents with ETA and pricing
  • Service booking with time-slot scheduling and live checkout
  • Provider job feed with accept/deny and Free/Occupied status
  • Provider earnings, ratings, and job-completion reports
  • Admin dashboard for locations, pricing, and billing
